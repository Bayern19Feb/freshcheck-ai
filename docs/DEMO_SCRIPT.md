# Two-Minute Demonstration Script — FreshCheck AI

*(About 2 minutes. Times are approximate. Hold the phone so the judge can see the screen.)*

**0:00 — Greeting**
"Good morning! My name is ______ and my project is **FreshCheck AI — a smarter way to check fruit freshness**."

**0:10 — Problem**
"A lot of fruit gets thrown away because people aren't sure if it's still good. And many people don't know the difference between a fruit that is **ripe** and one that is **spoiling**. Ripening is the fruit's own natural change. Spoiling is mostly caused by tiny microbes like mould and bacteria."

**0:30 — Live scan**
"Let me show you. I tap **Scan a Fruit**, put this banana inside the square, and tap **Capture**… then **Analyse photo**."
*(Wait for the result.)*
"The app says **______** with **____% model confidence**. It also shows which fruit it detected and gives advice."

*(Optional second scan with the spotted banana or apple.)*
"Now this spotted banana — see, it says **______**. Brown sugar spots are normal ripening; that banana is great for a milkshake!"

*If the result is Uncertain:* "The AI says **Uncertain**. That's on purpose — when its score is below our threshold, it doesn't guess. It tells you to check the fruit yourself."

**1:05 — How the AI works**
"I trained the AI with **Google Teachable Machine** using 3,600 labelled photos of fresh and spoiled fruit from public research datasets. The computer learned the patterns of colour and texture. The AI runs **on the phone** with TensorFlow.js, so the photo is never uploaded. I tested it with 720 photos it had never seen, and it was correct 98.6% of the time when it gave an answer — but those were dataset photos, so real fruit can be harder."

**1:30 — Benefits**
"It's free, private, works offline, and it teaches people to use ripe fruit instead of wasting it. There's also a learning centre and a quiz."

**1:40 — Limitations**
"But a photo only shows the outside. The app **cannot** see inside the fruit or detect germs, so it never says a fruit is safe to eat. Always look, smell, and ask an adult."

**1:55 — Closing**
"So AI can help us notice signs of freshness and reduce food waste — but it's a helper, not a judge. Thank you! Would you like to try scanning a fruit?"

---
Fill every blank with **real** numbers from your own results. Never say a number you did not measure.
